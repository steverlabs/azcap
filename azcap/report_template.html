Azure VM SKU restriction pressure

The score is a comparative restriction-pressure index, not a published Azure capacity percentage: 0 = no restrictions among assessed SKUs, 100 = fully restricted. A region-restricted SKU counts fully; a zone-restricted SKU counts as the fraction of its zones blocked (1 of 3 zones = 0.33). The region figure aggregates family scores using the weighting shown above. Quota-blocked SKUs are a quota or subscription-eligibility signal, so they are excluded; a family or region with no assessable SKUs is shown as n/a. Results are subscription-specific and do not guarantee deployment-time allocation.

Restriction pressure by region × family

Paired regions

Each requested region against its Microsoft-designated pair (from the Subscriptions API). "Both constrained" lists families with a restriction-pressure score ≥ 50 on both sides. Cross-geography pairs and pairs without availability zones are flagged because they matter for residency and for zone-resilient DR designs. Regions with no pair are newer regions Microsoft launched unpaired; DR there is a region you choose, not a pair.

Zone pressure

Number of SKUs with a zone-scoped restriction in each availability zone. Uneven counts across zones in a region are the early warning before region-level restriction.

Quota headroom

Compute vCPU quota per family (current use vs. limit). A 0 limit means the family is not provisioned for this subscription — request quota; a rejected or held request in a region is itself a capacity signal.
